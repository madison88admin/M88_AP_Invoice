1. System Overview

The AP Invoice Automation System manages invoice intake from upload through OCR extraction, validation, approval, exception resolution, accounting review, posting preparation, payment batching, and audit tracking.

Primary Business Goal

Reduce manual AP effort while keeping invoice decisions traceable, role-based, and supported by validation checks.

Core Capabilities

  • Invoice PDF upload and AI/OCR extraction.
  • Vendor, bank, signature, amount, and MPO validation.
  • Role-based approvals and accounting review.
  • Exception handling, on-hold tracking, and audit logs.

3. Navigation and Screens

The left sidebar is the main navigation surface. Menu items are role-aware, so users only see screens relevant to their access level.

4. Dashboard

The Dashboard gives each user a consolidated view of invoice volume, pending work, exceptions, SLA exposure, and approval activity.

Common Dashboard Actions

  • Use quick filters to focus on returned, urgent, or aging invoices.
  • Search by invoice number, vendor, MPO, brand, status, or category.
  • Select an invoice row to open the detail panel.
  • Use invoice detail tabs for overview, pipeline, validation, actions, and audit history.

5. Upload and OCR Review

The upload workflow creates invoice records from PDF files. The system extracts invoice fields, detects multi-invoice documents, validates PO/MPO references, and lets users correct extracted data before saving.

  1. Click Upload Invoice from the header or sidebar route.
  2. Select or drag a PDF into the upload area.
  3. Wait for extraction. The system may use PDF parsing plus available AI/OCR engines.
  4. Review vendor, invoice number, dates, amount, currency, category, brand, MPO, quantity, payment terms, bank, SWIFT, and account number.
  5. If multiple invoices are detected, review each split item before confirming.
  6. Save corrections when OCR extraction was wrong so future extraction can improve.
  7. Confirm the invoice to move it into validation and workflow routing.

6. Invoice Repository

The repository is the searchable historical store for invoice records. Use it when you need to find an invoice by vendor, invoice number, status, date, category, MPO, or payment stage.

Recommended Use

  • Start broad with date/status filters, then narrow by vendor or invoice number.
  • Open invoice details to view pipeline stage, validation result, corrections, exceptions, and audit history.
  • Use the repository for investigation rather than workflow-only task queues.

7. Validation and NextGen Checks

Validation protects the workflow from bad vendor data, duplicate invoices, missing approvals, mismatched bank details, incorrect amounts, and PO/MPO mismatches.

Dates and SLAInvoice date, due date, late submission aging.Confirm dates or resolve SLA exception.

Critical NextGen changes, such as amount, vendor, or PO mismatch, should create exceptions. Informational changes, such as quantity-only differences, may appear as notifications or warnings depending on workflow rules.

8. Approvals

The Approval Inbox shows invoices awaiting the current user’s action. Approval visibility depends on role, threshold, brand scope, and workflow stage.

  1. Open Approvals from the sidebar.
  2. Review invoice details, extracted fields, validation outcome, and any comments.
  3. Approve if the invoice is correct and within your authority.
  4. Reject if the invoice should not proceed. Always enter a clear rejection reason.
  5. Return for correction if the invoice can proceed after purchasing or accounting edits.

9. Exceptions and On-Hold Queue

Exceptions stop or slow the invoice until an authorized user resolves, waives, or corrects the underlying issue. The On-Hold Queue focuses on invoices held for batch threshold, validation, or accounting reasons.

Common Exception Types

  • AMOUNT_MISMATCH: Invoice amount differs from PO/MPO or expected amount.
  • PO_NOT_FOUND: MPO cannot be found in NextGen.
  • BANK_MISMATCH: Bank details differ from vendor master records.
  • DUPLICATE_INVOICE: Matching invoice number/vendor already exists.
  • MISSING_SIGNATURE: Required signatory was not detected or has not approved.
  • LATE_SUBMISSION: Invoice falls outside expected submission timing.

Resolve vs Waive

Resolve when the invoice data has been corrected or the issue is no longer present. Waive only when the issue is real but business-approved to proceed. Waivers should include a clear reason because they are part of the audit trail.

10. Accounting Review

Accounting Review is used after purchasing approval or when invoices are ready for posting/payment preparation. Accounting users should confirm all required signatures, validate bank details, review pre-post warnings, and check whether any override is needed.

  • Use Review for invoices in accounting-facing stages such as pending accounting, approved, posted, or paid.
  • Check the detail panel before posting or scheduling payment.
  • Use variance bypass only when authorized and documented.
  • Release invoices from hold only after the hold reason is addressed.

13. Vendor Management

Vendor Management stores supplier identity, aliases, bank details, and verification status. Bank updates are sensitive and should be requested and approved through the controlled workflow.

  • Search for existing vendors before creating a new vendor.
  • Keep aliases up to date so OCR/vendor matching stays accurate.
  • Verify bank details before allowing payment processing.
  • Use bank change requests instead of directly overwriting sensitive fields where approval is required.

14. Audit Logs

Audit logs preserve the who, what, when, and why of invoice processing. They are especially important for approvals, exceptions, waivers, bank changes, posting, payment completion, and user management.

Common Audit Events

  • User login and demo login.
  • Invoice upload, OCR confirmation, field correction, and validation.
  • Approval requested, approved, rejected, or returned.
  • Exception created, resolved, or waived.
  • Bank change requested, approved, or rejected.
  • Payment batch submitted, reviewed, exported, processed, failed, or cancelled.

16. Best Practices

For Purchasing

  • Review OCR carefully before confirming.
  • Correct vendor, amount, MPO, and bank data early.
  • Use clear rejection or return reasons.
  • Monitor aging and SLA warnings.

For Accounting

  • Confirm approvals and signatures before posting/payment steps.
  • Do not waive exceptions without business justification.
  • Attach payment proof and reference when processing batches.
  • Use audit logs to investigate unusual activity.

For Supervisors

  • Review variance bypasses and waivers regularly.
  • Monitor SLA analytics for bottlenecks.
  • Keep vendor bank verification disciplined.
  • Use reports to coach process improvement.

For IT/Admin

  • Maintain user roles and active status.
  • Confirm API, database, NextGen, and OCR engine health.
  • Preserve audit integrity and avoid direct data edits.
  • Update this manual when workflows change.
